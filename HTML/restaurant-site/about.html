About the Mess

Steam-powered provisions. Bulldog determination.

Meet the Major

Major McClellan believes an army marches on its stomach, but its supper ought to fly. This stubborn bulldog commands a steam-powered airship with one mission: deliver hot meals, fresh supplies, and a little comfort to troops on the front lines.

With brass goggles at the ready and a nose for good gravy, the Major keeps a watchful eye on every dispatch. His standards are simple: keep the boilers stoked, the kettles full, and the hardtack reasonably chewable.

Our Crew, Your Chow

Behind every delivery stands a resourceful crew of bulldog officers, human cooks, and animal mechanics. Together, they turn modest wartime rations into meals worth gathering around, from Boiler-Room Bean Pot to the General's Roast.

Whether your unit needs a quick ration drop or full mess service, Major McClellan's Migrating Mechanical Mess brings the galley to you. Listen for the engines, ready your mess tin, and follow the smell of supper.

Hot meals. Fresh supplies. Morale. MMMMM satisfaction.
